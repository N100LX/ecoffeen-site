About Emily

Horses have been part of Emily's life for decades. She shows American Saddlebreds and bought her first cutting horse in 1998. That lifelong time in the barn and the show ring shapes how she sees and photographs horses and the people who ride them.

Emily has studied equine photography in workshops with Tony Stromberg in California, Wyoming and Canada.
